PCB Mechanics Cheat Sheet

  • Frontline Triggering: Send ~5,000 Infantry to trigger captain defense/buff skills without crowding out your primary DPS troops.
  • Troop Crowding Law: Damage scales with diminishing returns as troop count grows. Stacking high-density DPS (Marksmen/Lancers) yields significantly higher damage returns than expanding raw march capacity.
  • Joiner Hard Cap: Only the top 4 rally joiners' 1st hero skills apply to the rally, capping out at a maximum +100% total damage boost (+25% each).
